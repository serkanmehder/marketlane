# MarketLane

A research platform for studying AI shopping decisions.

MarketLane combines an online shopping environment with an experimental framework for examining how AI agents evaluate products, compare prices, and make decisions on behalf of users. Agents interact with product offers, baskets, and checkout pages while the platform records their observable actions.

The aim is to study how agents process and respond to information through their observable behavior: what they encounter, how they compare alternatives, and how their decisions evolve during a task. This makes it possible to examine the path to a decision alongside the final choice.

## Platform capabilities

### A/B testing and experimentation

An integrated experimental framework supports randomized A/B tests and studies with multiple conditions. Researchers can compare how different information, presentations, and task instructions shape agent behavior within a controlled shopping environment.

### Behavioral tracking

Timestamped clickstream records capture navigation, selections, and interactions across the shopping journey. These records provide a view of how an agent moves through a task, explores available information, and arrives at a choice.

### Results and analysis

A dashboard brings together session activity and experimental outcomes. It supports group comparisons, statistical summaries, inspection of individual sessions, and data exports for further analysis.

### Automated agent sessions

Experiment runners support repeated agent sessions with configurable tasks and model settings. Runs are organized into separate batches, allowing researchers to manage studies and compare behavior across conditions and models.


## About this repository

This repository provides a public overview of the project. The application code, experiment runners, prompts, and research data are maintained privately.
